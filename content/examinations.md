Three routes matter to most Nigerian anaesthesia trainees, and each has its own page here: the fellowship examinations of the West African College of Surgeons, the fellowship examinations of the National Postgraduate Medical College of Nigeria, and diploma-level anaesthesia qualifications.

## How these pages work

Each page gives an orientation to the route and a study-planning framework. Examination structure, eligibility and dates change, so every page carries a last-verified date and points to the official college sources that govern. Where a page has not yet been verified against the official publication, it says so plainly rather than guessing.

No protected examination questions are reproduced anywhere on this site.
