anaesthesia.ng publishes educational material for anaesthesia trainees, practitioners and educators in Nigeria and West Africa.

## Review before publication

- Clinical articles are read by a practising anaesthetist before they go live.
- Every article shows its author, publication date and last-reviewed date.
- Demonstration entries are clearly labelled and are replaced after editorial review.

## Sources and dates

- Each article lists the references it rests on and the date it was last reviewed.
- Examination dates, fees and regulations come from the colleges; this site links to them rather than restating them.

## Corrections

- Errors reported by readers are corrected on the page, with the change noted.
- Material that cannot be verified is withdrawn rather than annotated.

## Scope

The material supports learning. It does not replace local protocols, supervision or clinical judgement, and no page on this site is medical advice for an individual patient.
