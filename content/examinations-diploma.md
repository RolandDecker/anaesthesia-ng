Diploma-level anaesthesia qualifications sit alongside fellowship training as a recognised route for doctors building anaesthesia competence. The awarding bodies, entry requirements and course structures differ, and candidates should rely on the awarding institution's own published information.

## Status of this page

This page will list the diploma routes relevant to Nigerian trainees, each with its official source, once the details have been verified against the awarding institutions' current publications. Until then it deliberately carries no specifics.

## Where verification will come from

- The awarding institution's official website and current prospectus.
- The National Postgraduate Medical College of Nigeria, which runs diploma programmes in select faculties: [npmcn.edu.ng](https://npmcn.edu.ng/)
