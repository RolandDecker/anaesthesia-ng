## The idea in one sentence

Pre-oxygenation replaces the nitrogen in the patient's functional residual capacity with oxygen, creating an oxygen reservoir that buys time between induction and a secured airway.

## Practical points commonly examined

- Adequate pre-oxygenation is usually defined by an end-tidal oxygen fraction above a threshold rather than by time alone.
- Positioning, a well-fitted mask and a tight seal matter more than flow rate.
- Special situations, including pregnancy and obesity, reduce safe apnoea time and deserve explicit attention in viva answers.

This is a teaching scaffold, not a protocol. Local institutional guidance always governs practice.
