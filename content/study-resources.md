This section will hold reading lists, structured study guides and downloadable aids for trainees at every stage, from first rotation to fellowship preparation.

## Planned for the first content releases

- Reading lists organised by the site's twelve clinical categories.
- A study-planning guide for each examination route, linked from the examinations section.
- Downloadable one-page summaries, each dated and attributed.

Nothing is published in this section yet. When guides appear they will carry a last-reviewed date and, where clinical, the name of the reviewer.
