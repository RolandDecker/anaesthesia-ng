anaesthesia.ng is an independent academic and professional knowledge hub for anaesthesia in Nigeria and Africa. It exists to make high-quality educational material easy to find for anaesthesia residents, medical students, doctors, practitioners, researchers and the departments that train them.

## Editorial governance

- Clinical material is reviewed by a qualified human before publication.
- Every article shows its author, publication date and last-reviewed date.
- Educational articles, opinion and formal guidance from professional bodies are clearly distinguished.
- Material that falls out of date is flagged for review or archived.
- Nothing here replaces clinical judgement, institutional protocols or specialist consultation.

## Ownership and independence

The site is owned and controlled by its founder. It carries no advertising in this version and endorses no commercial product. Institutional endorsements are never implied.
