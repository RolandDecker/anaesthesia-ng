The West African College of Surgeons (WACS) awards postgraduate surgical qualifications across West Africa, and anaesthesia is one of its faculties. Trainees progress through primary and final fellowship examinations during accredited residency training.

## Status of this page

This page is a navigation and study-planning scaffold. Its detailed sections on examination structure, eligibility and syllabus are being prepared and will carry a last-verified date once checked against the college's own current publications.
