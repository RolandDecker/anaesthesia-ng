The simplest way to reach the editorial team is by email; a dedicated address will be published here once it is configured.

## What to contact us about

- Corrections to any published article; corrections are published transparently.
- Suggestions for topics, especially examination and research content.
- Permissions questions about reuse of material.

We do not provide clinical advice by email. Clinical questions belong with your own supervising consultant or institutional channels.
