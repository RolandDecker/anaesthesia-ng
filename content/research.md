Research is where anaesthesia in Africa grows. This section collects the three things a trainee or consultant needs most: skills, opportunities and a view of the regional literature.

- [Research methodology and skills](/research/methodology/) covers study design, biostatistics, critical appraisal, scientific writing and ethics.
- [Opportunities](/research/opportunities/) will list grants, fellowships, conferences and calls for papers as they are verified.
- [Research in Africa](/research/africa/) frames the regional picture and the questions worth answering here.

Nothing in this section carries fabricated statistics or invented collaborations. Listings appear only after their official source has been checked.
