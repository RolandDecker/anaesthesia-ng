anaesthesia.ng is a static website. In this version it sets no analytics, stores no accounts and collects no personal data.

## What this means in practice

- Browsing generates only the standard server logs kept by the hosting provider.
- The search function runs entirely in your browser; your query is not sent to any server.
- No patient data, medical histories or sensitive clinical information is collected, ever.

If a contact form or analytics tool is added later, this page will be updated first to describe exactly what is collected and why. Nothing here should be read as a legal compliance statement.
