Human-reviewed clinical material

Clinical content is checked by a practising anaesthetist before publication, and every article shows what it rests on.

Read the editorial policy
Reviewed before publication
A practising anaesthetist reads each clinical article before it goes live.
Sources and dates shown
Each article lists its references and the date it was last reviewed.
Corrections made in the open
Errors reported by readers are corrected on the page, with the change noted.
Education, not instruction
The material supports learning. It does not replace local protocols, supervision or your own clinical judgement.
